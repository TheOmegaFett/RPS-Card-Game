# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Being Respectful**: Using welcoming and inclusive language
- **Being Constructive**: Providing and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- **Being Collaborative**: Focusing on what is best for the community
- **Showing Empathy**: Being kind toward other people and their perspectives
- **Being Professional**: Demonstrating professionalism in all interactions

### Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Project communication channels
- Project events or representations
- Social media when representing the project

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by:

- Opening a private issue
- Contacting the project maintainer directly via GitHub

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

### Consequences

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Consequences may include:

1. **Warning**: A private written warning
2. **Temporary Ban**: Temporary ban from community spaces
3. **Permanent Ban**: Permanent ban from the community

## Academic Context

This project was developed as part of academic learning and uses AI assistance tools. We expect all contributors to:

- Maintain academic integrity standards
- Be transparent about use of AI or other assistance
- Take responsibility for understanding and validating all contributed code
- Properly attribute sources and collaborations
